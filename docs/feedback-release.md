# Post-launch evidence release

This release responds to the first review of the deployed portfolio by shifting
the experience from product intention toward inspectable proof.

## Implemented

- RontgenAI is the flagship and is labeled `Public preview`.
- Its case study links to seven public v1 product surfaces and separates live
  evidence from work still in development.
- Every project has a distinct narrative trace:
  - Dev Pulse AI follows one article through research and approval.
  - RontgenAI follows a repository through mapping and review.
  - CognoraAI follows a learner as feedback changes a knowledge graph.
  - OrkestriaAI follows an autonomous run into a human approval state.
- Every case study includes an Engineering Evidence chapter covering
  architecture, AI, data, reliability, security, trade-offs and deployment.
- Every case study includes a curated, expandable real-product gallery. The 22
  selected captures are optimized from approximately 25 MB of source PNGs to
  approximately 1.5 MB of WebP assets.
- Contribution lists make Jabir's role explicit.
- The homepage includes a professional timeline, personal principle,
  exploration list and compact Build Log.
- `/lab` contains three deterministic browser experiments.
- `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` opens the accessible System Navigator.
- Visitors can reduce motion or disable WebGL effects from the command menu.
- The contact section now explains best-fit work, the minimum useful brief,
  supports copying the email address and gives a clearer success next step.
- Vercel Speed Insights is mounted for real-user performance data.

## Evidence boundaries

The portfolio does not invent adoption figures, repository-size limits,
employment dates, private infrastructure details or public repositories.
RontgenAI's public product count and product-surface descriptions come from the
current public preview. Other projects retain explicit prototype, product
design or interaction-prototype labels.

## Assets still requiring Jabir's source material

The following feedback items require authentic media or private career data and
should not be fabricated:

- A 45–60 second RontgenAI walkthrough recording.
- Public repository URLs.
- Employer names, dates, team size and measurable career impact where
  confidentiality allows.
- A portrait, desk photograph, introduction recording, résumé file or calendar
  link.

The remaining assets can be added without changing the storytelling model.
